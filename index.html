Privacy Policy for Tekan Bijak

Effective date: October 15, 2026

1. Introduction

Tekan Bijak is an educational application designed for children aged 3–5 years. It provides basic learning activities, including ABC, numbers, Alif Ba Ta, and syllables.

We respect the privacy of our users, especially children.

2. Information Collection

Tekan Bijak does not collect, request, or store personal information from users. The app does not require an account or registration.

3. Internet Access

Tekan Bijak works entirely offline and does not require an internet connection to access its learning features.

4. Advertising and Third-Party Services

Tekan Bijak does not display advertisements and does not integrate third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services.

5. Children's Privacy

Tekan Bijak is designed for children aged 3–5 years. We do not knowingly collect personal information through the app.

6. Data Sharing

We do not share personal information with third parties.

7. Data Security

The app operates offline and does not transmit personal information to our servers.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when necessary. Updated versions will be published at this URL.

9. Contact Us

For questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact the developer through the contact information provided on the Tekan Bijak Google Play Store listing.
